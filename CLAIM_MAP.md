## Project-level boundary

The trilogy does **not** presently establish:

- a universal conservation law for information;
- a physical hidden variable beyond quantum mechanics;
- universal injectivity or complete dynamical observability;
- universal invariant discovery;
- confirmatory seismic prediction;
- general transfer across real physical systems.

Its established contribution is a linked methodology: locate the map that merges a distinction, test whether the merged distinction matters, and make representational repair an auditable response to that failure.
